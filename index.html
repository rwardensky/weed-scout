How scores work

Deal: the product's lowest available price per gram divided into the median $/g of in-stock S/A products in the same type and size band (≤1g, up to 3.5g, larger; flower bands are eighth, up to half ounce, ounce and up). +30% means 30% cheaper than typical. Quality (0–100): vendor tier (S 40, A+ 36, A 32, B 20), +12 for a known hashmaker (710, Bryantist, HRC, Stayfly…) or +6 for another third-party brand, up to +20 from site star ratings (weighted toward 4.3 when there are few reviews), ±10 from vendor Reddit sentiment, and up to ±8 when the strain itself is discussed. Overall: quality × √deal, so a great jar at a fair price beats a mediocre jar at a steep discount.
