How scores work

Price with shipping: for each size, the price plus that store's shipping if the order is under its free-shipping minimum. When a store doesn't publish its terms, it assumes $10 shipping, free over $100, and marks it "est." The cheapest size per gram after shipping is what's shown and ranked. Deal: that shipped $/g against the median shipped $/g of in-stock S/A products of the same type and size band (≤1g, up to 3.5g, larger; flower bands are eighth, up to half ounce, ounce and up). +30% means 30% cheaper than typical. Quality (0–100): vendor tier (S 40, A+ 36, A 32, B 20), +12 for a known hashmaker (710, Bryantist, HRC, Stayfly…) or +6 for another third-party brand, up to +20 from site star ratings (weighted toward 4.3 when there are few reviews), ±10 from vendor Reddit sentiment (weighted toward upvoted, recent posts; "not fire" counts as negative), and up to ±8 when the strain itself is discussed. Overall: quality × √deal, so a great jar at a fair price beats a mediocre jar at a steep discount. Changes: prices are recorded every refresh. "↓" marks a drop in the last 3 days, "30-day low" the cheapest it has been in a month.
