How scores work

Price with shipping: for each size, the price plus that store's shipping if the order is under its free-shipping minimum. When a store doesn't publish its terms, it assumes $10 shipping, free over $100, and marks it "est." The cheapest size per gram after shipping is what's shown and ranked. Deal: that shipped $/g against the median shipped $/g of in-stock S/A products of the same type and size band (≤1g, up to 3.5g, larger; flower bands are eighth, up to half ounce, ounce and up). +30% means 30% cheaper than typical. Quality (0–100): vendor tier (S 40, A+ 36, A 32, B 20), +12 for a known hashmaker (710, Bryantist, HRC, Stayfly…) or +6 for another third-party brand, up to +20 from site star ratings (weighted toward 4.3 when there are few reviews), ±10 from vendor Reddit sentiment (weighted toward upvoted, recent posts; "not fire" counts as negative), and up to ±8 when the strain itself is discussed. Overall: quality × √deal, so a great jar at a fair price beats a mediocre jar at a steep discount. Promo codes: when a store's homepage advertises a code or sitewide sale with a percent off (not first-order-only), "Apply promo codes" takes it off the price before shipping. Codes are read automatically and can expire, so check at checkout. Your ratings: 👍 adds 15 to that product's quality and 👎 takes off 30; your average thumbs per store nudge all its products by up to ±6. These only change your view. THCa: the percentage the store lists in its title or description (not read from lab PDFs). "Most THCa per $" ranks by milligrams of THCa per dollar. Changes: prices are recorded every refresh. "↓" marks a drop in the last 3 days, "30-day low" the cheapest it has been in a month.
